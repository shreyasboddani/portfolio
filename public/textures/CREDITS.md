# Scanned surface textures

Downloaded from Poly Haven, under its CC0 public-domain dedication. The local
1K JPGs are optimized derivatives; the Blender build packs these maps into the
editable source and exports WebP textures in the GLB.

- [Wood Table Worn](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_table_worn), photography by
  Dimitrios Savva, processing by Rico Cilliers: desktop, frame and wood details.
- [Blue Plaster Wall](https://polyhaven.com/a/blue_plaster_wall): wall relief
  and roughness, with the room's own blue paint color.
- [Brown Leather](https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_leather): leather relief and
  roughness, with the desk mat's own green color.

The photographs, screenshots and resume elsewhere in the portfolio belong to
Shreyas Boddani and are separate from these texture licenses.
